This CPU architecture is the continuation of a previous, less developed model I just finished. The previous design was an important base to start, so that I could have a general idea of how the architecture was going to develop, and it greatly helped tackling down many problems that I knew I was going to have, as well as showed defects or possible upgrades which will mostly be covered on this new, probably final, version. Most of the information about the previous model is on the [[Legacy CPU]] section, so that the work of this model is less cluttered.
